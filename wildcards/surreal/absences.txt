# Absence and concealment. Something that must be there is missing, or is covered. Deadpan, never gory.
the face of THE SUBJECT is covered by a plain white cloth, tied loosely at the neck
THE SUBJECT is seen only from behind, and so is its reflection in the mirror ahead
a shadow of THE SUBJECT lies across the wall, and THE SUBJECT itself is not in the room
the clothes of THE SUBJECT stand upright and filled out with nobody inside them
THE SUBJECT has been cut out of the scene like a paper shape, and the hole shows clear sky
everything in the room is present except THE SUBJECT, whose outline remains in the dust
footprints lead to where THE SUBJECT should be standing and stop
the chair keeps the dent of THE SUBJECT, who is not there
THE SUBJECT is wrapped entirely in brown paper and string, its shape exact
a painted portrait shows the room behind THE SUBJECT, accurately, and no sitter
the mirror reflects the whole room faithfully and leaves out THE SUBJECT
THE SUBJECT holds an object in front of its face that hides it exactly
a rectangular section of THE SUBJECT is missing, and through it the background continues undisturbed
